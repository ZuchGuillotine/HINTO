Deleting your HINTO data

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE - TBD]

You can delete your HINTO account and all the data attached to it at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what happens when you do. It also applies if you signed in with Facebook or Apple.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open HINTO and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Email us

Send an email to support@hinto.app with the subject "Delete my HINTO account" from the email address on your account. If you signed in with Facebook or Apple and don't know which email is on the account, tell us the name or username you used and we'll verify another way. We complete emailed requests within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you signed in with Facebook

Removing HINTO from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Apps and websites) stops HINTO from accessing your Facebook account, but it does not delete your HINTO data. Use one of the options above to delete your HINTO account as well. HINTO does not post to Facebook or store your Facebook friends list.

If you signed in with Apple

You can stop using Sign in with Apple for HINTO from your Apple ID settings. As with Facebook, that alone doesn't delete your HINTO data - use one of the options above.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email, Apple or Facebook link)
  • Your profile (name, username, bio, age, photo)
  • Your list of situationships and all notes
  • Every vote session you created, including all votes and comments friends left on them
  • Your AI coach conversations
  • Your block list

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, then it is gone.
  • Reports: if your account was reported, or you reported someone, we may keep the report record (not your list or messages) for up to 12 months to act on repeat abuse.
  • Server logs: technical logs that may include your IP address are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Votes you cast on other people's lists as an anonymous voter are not linked to your account and are not affected.

Deleting just some data

You don't have to delete everything. In the app you can remove individual list entries, end vote sessions, and edit or clear your profile at any time. To delete a specific coach conversation, email us and we'll remove it.

Questions

Email support@hinto.app. Our Privacy Policy has the full picture of what we collect and why.
