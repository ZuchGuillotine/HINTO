Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE - TBD] · Last updated: [DATE - TBD]

HINTO ("we", "us") is a private app for keeping a ranked list of the people you're seeing, letting friends you choose vote on it, and talking things through with an AI coach. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed. If anything is unclear, email support@hinto.app.

1. Who can use HINTO

HINTO is for people aged 16 and older. We ask for your age when you create an account. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete it. If you believe a user is under 16, report it in the app or email us.

DataWhere it comes fromWhy we use it

3. How the AI coach uses your data

When you send a message to the coach, we send that message, your recent messages in the same conversation, and relevant context from your list (entry names, notes, and vote results) to OpenAI to generate a reply. We do not send your email address. OpenAI processes this data under its API terms and, per its published policy, does not use API data to train its models. Coach replies are automated and are not medical, legal, or mental-health advice.

4. Who we share data with

  • Supabase - hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
  • OpenAI - generates AI coach responses (see section 3).
  • Apple and Meta - only if you choose to sign in with them; they tell us who you are and we do not post anything to your accounts.
  • Email delivery provider - sends your sign-in codes.
  • Your invited friends - anyone with a vote link you share can see the entries on your list and, if you chose named votes, the names other voters entered. Vote results are visible only to you.
  • Legal requests - we disclose data when required by law or to protect users from harm.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. What your friends see

Your list is private by default. It becomes visible only to people who open a vote link you created and shared, and only for as long as that vote is open. Voters see entry names, emoji, and notes - not your bio, email, or coach conversations. Voters never see other people's votes.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data, lists, votes, and coach conversations: until you delete them or your account.
  • Vote sessions: votes remain attached to your account after a session closes until you delete the account.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after resolution, so we can act on repeat behaviour.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: up to 30 days after deletion, after which deleted data is gone from backups too.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and list at any time in the app.
  • End a vote at any time from your results screen.
  • Block and report people from Settings or from a comment.
  • Delete your account in Settings, or by emailing support@hinto.app. See Data deletion for what happens.
  • Access or export your data by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the UK, EEA, or a US state with a privacy law, you may have additional rights (access, correction, portability, restriction, objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority). Email us to exercise them.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our hosting provider. Sign-in uses one-time codes or Apple/Meta sign-in rather than passwords. Access to production data is limited to the people who operate the service. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a breach that affects you, we will tell you.
