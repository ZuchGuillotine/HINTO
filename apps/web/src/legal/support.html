Support

We're a small team. Email is the fastest way to reach us.

Email us

support@hinto.app - we aim to reply within 2 business days. Write from the email address on your account so we can verify it's you.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code.

Check spam and promotions folders, wait a minute, then tap "Send a new code". Codes expire after a short time. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with the address you used.

My friend's vote link says it has ended.

Vote links are open for a limited time set by the list owner (up to a week) and she can end them early. Ask her for a new link.

Can I vote twice?

No. Each person gets one vote per list so results stay honest. If you voted by mistake, tell the list owner.

Who can see my list?

Only you, unless you create and share a vote link. People with the link see the entries while voting is open. They never see other people's votes or your coach conversations.

Someone is being abusive.

Use Report under any vote comment, or Report a profile in Settings. You can also block accounts from Settings. For anything urgent, email us with "URGENT" in the subject.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Or email us from your account address. Details are on the Data deletion page.

The coach said something unhelpful or upsetting.

Coach replies are automated and can be wrong. Please forward the conversation to us so we can improve it. If you're in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line - the coach is not a substitute.

Report a safety concern

If you believe a HINTO user is under 16, or you see content that involves exploitation, threats, or self-harm, email support@hinto.app immediately with any links or usernames you have. We review these reports first.
