Support

HNNT Support

How to reach us, report a problem, and manage your account.

Contact support

Email support@hnnt.app from the email address on your account. To help us respond quickly, include:

  • Your HNNT username.
  • Whether you are using the iPhone app or the web app, and the version if you know it.
  • What you were trying to do, what happened instead, and a screenshot if you have one.

We read every message. Account, safety, and deletion requests are handled first.

Report a user or content

If someone is harassing you, impersonating someone, posting content about a person without their consent, or otherwise breaking the Acceptable Use Policy, you can report it:

  1. In the app: open Settings and choose Report a user or content. On the web, feed posts and vote comments also have a Report button that fills the form in for you.
  2. Pick what you are reporting, choose a reason, and add any detail that helps us understand what happened.
  3. Submit. Reports are private. The person you report is not told who reported them.

You can also email support@hnnt.app with the username of the person and a description of what happened.

To stop someone from interacting with you, block them. Blocked users are listed under Settings → Blocked users, where you can unblock them at any time.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account, then confirm.
  • By email: send a message from the email address on your account to support@hnnt.app with the subject line Delete my account and your username.

Step-by-step instructions and a description of exactly what is removed are on the data deletion page.

Minimum age

HNNT is for people who are 16 or older. We do not knowingly keep an account or personal information for anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email support@hnnt.app and we will investigate and delete it.

If you are in danger

HNNT and its coach are not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. In the US you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) at any time.
