Your data

Delete your HNNT account and data

How to delete your account, what is removed, and how long it takes.

What deletion removes

When you delete your account, the following is removed from our live systems immediately:

  • Your profile: username, display name, bio, avatar, privacy settings, and linked sign-in providers (Apple, Meta, Snapchat, TikTok, email).
  • Your lists: every situationship, its notes, photos, and ranking.
  • Votes and voting sessions you created, and the votes and comments you left on friends' sessions.
  • Feed posts and friend connections.
  • Coach conversations: every message you sent to the coach and every reply.
  • Sessions: all sign-in tokens on every device are revoked, so you are signed out everywhere.

Deleted data is not recoverable. If you sign up again later you start with a new, empty account.

Backups

Our database keeps automated backups for disaster recovery. Backups are retained for 7 days and are then discarded, so any copy of your data inside a backup expires within 7 days of deletion. Backups are never used to restore an individual account.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to HNNT on the web at hnnt.app or in the iPhone app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Yes, delete my account.
  5. You are signed out and returned to the home page. Your data is gone at that moment.

Delete by email

If you can no longer sign in, email us instead:

  1. From the email address on your account, write to support@hnnt.app.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account and include your username.
  3. We reply to confirm the request came from you, then delete the account and confirm by email.

Signed in with Facebook or Instagram?

Removing HNNT from your Facebook or Instagram Apps and Websites settings stops HNNT from using that login. To delete the HNNT account itself and its data, use one of the two methods above. Deleting your HNNT account also removes the record that linked it to your Meta account.

What we may keep

We keep a minimal record of the deletion itself (that an account with this identifier was deleted and when) so we can honor it and answer any follow-up. Reports you filed about other users are kept so we can act on them, with your identity removed. Anything we are legally required to retain, such as billing records, is kept only as long as the law requires. See the Data Retention section of the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Email support@hnnt.app. See also the support page, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms of Service.
