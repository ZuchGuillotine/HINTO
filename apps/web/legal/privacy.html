1. Introduction

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (“HINTO,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the HINTO mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). HINTO is a social relationship coaching and voting platform that allows users to manage ranked lists of romantic interests, invite trusted friends to vote and comment, and receive personalized guidance from an AI relationship coach.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, the choices and rights you have, and the steps we take to protect it. By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

We are headquartered in Seattle, Washington, United States. This Policy applies to all users of the Service worldwide, including users in the European Economic Area (“EEA”) and the United Kingdom (“UK”).

2. Who Can Use HINTO

HINTO is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a user under 16, we will delete that information promptly. If you believe a user under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

Because HINTO involves sensitive social and relationship content, we recommend that users between 16 and 18 review this Policy with a parent or guardian. We rely on self-attestation of age at sign-up and reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that misrepresent the user’s age.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

3.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, date of birth or age confirmation, profile photo, and any optional bio or social handles you choose to provide.
  • Authentication identifiers from third-party providers you use to sign in or link your account, including Apple, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google, Snapchat, and TikTok. These providers may share with us a unique user ID, your name, email address, profile picture, and, where you authorize it, your friends list or connections list.
  • Account preferences, language, notification settings, and similar configuration data.

3.2 Profile and Relationship Content (Sensitive)

Because of HINTO’s purpose, certain content you provide is inherently sensitive. We treat the following as Sensitive Information and apply heightened safeguards:

  • Situationships: names, photos, nicknames, descriptions, ranks, notes, status (e.g., active, archived), and any other details you record about romantic or dating interests.
  • Voting sessions: rankings, comments, reactions, and free-text feedback that you or your invited friends submit.
  • AI coach conversations: messages you send to the AI coach and the responses it generates, which may include disclosures about your relationships, emotions, or personal life.
  • Friends and contacts data: lists of friends, followers, or connections we obtain from third-party social providers when you choose to use friend-voting features.

3.3 Device, Usage, and Technical Information

Automatically collected when you use the Service:

  • Device type, operating system, device identifiers, app version, and crash logs.
  • IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser type, referring URLs, and language settings.
  • Usage data such as screens viewed, features used, buttons tapped, session duration, and timestamps.
  • Cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies (web only). See Section 11 below.

3.4 Communications With Us

If you contact us through email, support forms, or in-app messaging, we keep a record of that correspondence and any information you choose to share.

3.5 Payment Information

If you purchase a subscription or in-app product, payment information is collected and processed by the relevant payment processor (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play, or our web payment processor). We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. We receive limited transaction information such as a transaction ID, product purchased, amount, currency, and renewal status.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, including age verification and provider linking.
  • Operate core features, including situationship lists, friend voting sessions, comments, and reactions.
  • Provide the AI relationship coach by sending relevant context to our AI subprocessors (see Section 6).
  • Personalize your experience and improve in-app recommendations and prompts.
  • Send service communications, such as security alerts, terms updates, transactional receipts, and support replies.
  • Send marketing communications about HINTO and our other first-party products, subject to your preferences and applicable law. You can opt out at any time (see Section 9).
  • Maintain trust and safety, including detecting and responding to harassment, stalking, impersonation, fraud, and other policy violations.
  • Conduct analytics, debugging, product research, and to improve and develop new features.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA / UK Users)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, we rely on the following legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the UK GDPR:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you have signed up for, including account creation, voting sessions, and the AI coach.
  • Consent: for optional features that involve Sensitive Information, marketing communications where required by law, accessing friends lists from social providers, and participation in the HAIDAA research platform (see Section 8).
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and improve the Service, prevent abuse, conduct analytics, and communicate with you about our own products, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws, court orders, and regulatory requests.

8. HAIDAA Research Platform (Opt-In)

HAIDAA is our research and data platform that explores health-related correlations across our suite of applications. Participation in HAIDAA is strictly opt-in. You will be presented with a separate, clear consent flow that explains what data may be included, how it will be processed, and how you can withdraw.

If you opt in to HAIDAA:

  • We may include de-identified or pseudonymized data derived from your HINTO activity in HAIDAA research datasets.
  • We may combine HAIDAA inputs with data from other applications you have opted into.
  • We will not publish information that identifies you personally.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time from the privacy settings in the app; withdrawal stops future processing for HAIDAA but does not affect research already completed.

9. Marketing Communications

With your permission where required by law, we may send marketing emails, push notifications, or in-app messages about HINTO features, related HINTO products, and our other first-party applications. We do not share your personal information with third parties for those third parties’ own direct marketing.

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Updating your notification preferences in app settings.
  • Contacting us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

Even if you opt out of marketing, we will still send you transactional and service-related messages (e.g., security alerts, receipts, policy updates).

10. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our users. Specific retention practices include:

  • Account and profile data: retained for the life of the account.
  • Situationships and voting content: retained until you delete the content or close the account; backups may persist for up to 90 days.
  • AI coach conversations: retained for the life of the account unless you delete individual conversations or your account; we may retain de-identified excerpts for safety and quality review.
  • Logs, analytics, and security records: typically retained 12–24 months.
  • Records required by law (e.g., tax, billing): retained as required by applicable law.

On the web version of the Service, we use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality (such as authentication and session management), performance analytics, and remembering preferences. 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Portability: receive a portable copy of certain information you provided to us.
  • Restriction / Objection: ask us to limit or stop processing of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (Settings → Privacy) or by contacting us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within the time frames required by applicable law (generally within 30 days for GDPR requests).

You also have the ability to delete your account at any time from the app, which will trigger deletion of your account data in accordance with Section 10.

13. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, encryption at rest for sensitive fields, access controls, row-level security in our database, monitoring, and employee training. No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. If we learn of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulators as required by law.
